Common questions

My alarm didn't ring. What should I check?

Make sure Upbeam is allowed to set alarms (Settings › Apps › Upbeam › Alarms), your iPhone was charged and on, and the alarm is switched on in the app. Also check your iPhone's alarm volume in Settings › Sounds & Haptics: it's the ceiling for every alarm. Our guide iPhone alarm didn't go off? covers more.

Will Upbeam ring on Silent or in a Focus?

Yes. Upbeam uses Apple's AlarmKit on iOS 26 and later, so alarms ring on the Lock Screen through Silent mode and Focus, even when the app is closed.

What if I turn the alarm off and fall back asleep?

When you set the alarm, turn on Backup alarms under If I'm not up. If you haven't finished your wake-up mission, Upbeam rings again 10, 15 and 20 minutes later, louder each time. With Premium, the Still up? check (Settings › After you're up) also rings gently 5 minutes after your mission and rings your alarm again if you don't answer.

How do I change the wake-up mission or its difficulty?

Edit the alarm and choose a mission under Wake-up mission, then pick Easy, Medium or Hard. Math is free; Typing, Steps and Barcode come with Premium.

The Barcode or Steps mission won't work.

These need the camera or Motion & Fitness. Allow them in Settings › Apps › Upbeam. If they aren't available while the alarm is ringing, the mission offers Use Math instead, so you can always turn the alarm off.

How do I use Nightstand?

Tap Nightstand on the home screen, or, with Upbeam open, put your iPhone on the charger on its side. It follows your phone: landscape on its side, portrait when you hold it upright. It turns light at sunrise and dark with the moon at sunset where you are (6 AM and 6 PM if you don't share your rough location). Tap anywhere to switch the red night light on or off; swipe up or down, or swipe up from the bottom edge, to leave. If an alarm rings while it's open, big Stop and Snooze buttons fill the screen. Nightstand is free.

How loud will my alarm be?

Your iPhone's Ringtone and Alerts volume (Settings › Sounds & Haptics) is the most any alarm can ring, and apps can't change it. When you set an alarm, tap Test my alarm to hear it for real 5 seconds later. Turning off Change with Buttons on the same screen stops the side buttons lowering it by accident.

I bought Premium on another iPhone. How do I restore it?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then open Upbeam › Settings › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Upbeam. Lifetime is a single payment, so there's nothing to cancel.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Upbeam needs iOS 26 or later on iPhone. See our privacy policy.
