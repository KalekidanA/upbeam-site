Common questions

My alarm didn't ring. What should I check?

Make sure Upbeam has alarm permission (Settings › Apps › Upbeam), your iPhone was charged and on, and the alarm is switched on in the app. Our guide iPhone alarm didn't go off? covers volume and other system settings.

Will Upbeam ring on Silent or in a Focus?

Yes. Upbeam uses Apple's AlarmKit on iOS 26 and later, so alarms sound through Silent mode and Focus.

I bought Lifetime on another iPhone. How do I restore it?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Upbeam › Settings › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Upbeam.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The math mission is too hard (or too easy).

Edit the alarm, tap Mission and choose a difficulty.
