iPhone alarm didn't go off? 9 checks to make sure it rings

6 min read · Updated November 2026 · Written for iOS 26

Fix it in 2 minutes
  • Settings › Sounds & Haptics: raise the Ringtone and Alerts slider. That's the alarm volume.
  • Settings › Face ID & Attention: turn off Attention Aware Features if your alarm gets quiet when you look at it.
  • Clock › Alarms: check the alarm is on, the time and AM/PM are right, and the sound isn't None.

Few things feel worse than waking up to daylight and realising your alarm never rang. The good news: in most cases, it's a setting, not a broken phone. Work through these checks tonight, then test with an alarm two minutes from now.

Myth: "My phone was on Silent, so the alarm didn't ring." Apple says the Ring/Silent switch (or Action button set to Silent) and Do Not Disturb don't affect the Clock app's alarm sound. The alarm still sounds. If yours didn't, the cause is somewhere below.

1. Check the alarm volume (the most common cause)

The Clock app's alarm doesn't have its own volume. It uses your Ringtone and Alerts volume. If that slider is low, your alarm will be too.

  1. Open Settings › Sounds & Haptics.
  2. Under Ringtone and Alerts, drag the slider to the right. A sound plays as you drag, so you can hear the level.

Note that media volume (what you hear in videos and music) is a separate level from Ringtone and Alerts volume, so a loud video doesn't mean a loud alarm.

2. Turn off Attention Aware Features

On iPhones with Face ID, Attention Aware Features lower the volume of alerts when the phone sees you looking at it. That can make an alarm go quiet the moment you pick up your phone, or seem silent. In 2024, Apple acknowledged an issue where some alarms didn't play a sound, and many people found that turning this off helped.

  1. Open Settings › Face ID & Attention.
  2. Turn off Attention Aware Features.

3. Make sure the sound isn't set to None

If your iPhone only vibrated, the alarm's sound may be set to None. Open Clock › Alarms, tap Edit, tap the alarm, tap Sound and pick a tone. Choose one of the built-in tones rather than a song: songs depend on your music library and can fail if a track isn't available offline.

4. Double-check time, AM/PM and repeat days

It sounds obvious, but it's the cause more often than people admit. In Clock › Alarms, confirm the switch is on (green), the time says AM when you mean morning, and the Repeat days include tomorrow. One-time alarms switch themselves off after they ring.

5. Check your Sleep schedule alarm separately

If you set a wake-up time through Sleep in the Health app, it appears at the top of the Clock app's Alarms tab and has its own settings. Editing your other alarms doesn't change it. If that's the alarm that failed, open it from the Clock app (or Health › Browse › Sleep) and check that it's on for tomorrow, with a sound and a volume set.

6. Don't change the date or time by hand

Apple notes that manually changing your iPhone's time in Settings, for example to speed up a game timer, isn't recommended and can affect alarms. Go to Settings › General › Date & Time and turn on Set Automatically.

7. Check you didn't stop it in your sleep

If your lock screen says an alarm was snoozed or stopped and you don't remember doing it, you probably did it half asleep. iOS 26 made the Stop and Snooze buttons larger, which made accidental stops easier. iOS 26.1 changed Stop to a Slide to stop gesture, while Snooze remains a tap. Updating to the latest iOS helps, and so does putting your phone where you have to stand up to reach it.

8. Update and restart

Alarm bugs do get fixed in updates. Go to Settings › General › Software Update and install anything available. Then restart your iPhone, set a test alarm for two minutes from now and lock the phone.

9. Reset all settings (last resort)

If alarms still fail, go to Settings › General › Transfer or Reset iPhone › Reset › Reset All Settings. This resets settings such as Wi-Fi passwords and wallpaper, but it doesn't delete your photos, apps or data. If even that doesn't work, contact Apple Support, as there may be a hardware problem with the speaker.

Before an important morning

  • Set two alarms a few minutes apart, so a single failure doesn't cost you.
  • Test the volume the night before by setting a 1-minute alarm.
  • Keep your phone charging. A dead battery is the one thing no alarm survives.

Frequently asked questions

Does the iPhone alarm work in Do Not Disturb or Sleep Focus?

Yes. Apple says Do Not Disturb doesn't affect the alarm sound, and the same goes for Focus modes, including Sleep.

Will my alarm play through my AirPods?

According to Apple, when headphones are connected the alarm plays through the iPhone's built-in speakers as well as the headphones, so you'll still hear it if you take them out.

Does the alarm ring if my iPhone is off?

No. If your iPhone is powered off or the battery is dead, the alarm won't ring. Keep it charged overnight.
