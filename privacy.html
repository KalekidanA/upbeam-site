Privacy policy

Effective date: to be set at launch

In plain wordsUpbeam has no account, shows no ads and doesn't sell or share your data. Your alarms, missions and wake-up history are stored on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your alarms, alarm settings, mission results and wake-up history are stored only on your device. We don't have a server that receives them, so we can't see them. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too.

Anonymous usage counts

To find bugs and understand which features are used, Upbeam may send anonymous, aggregated usage events (for example, "an alarm was created" or "the app crashed"). These events contain no name, email, contacts, location, alarm times or advertising identifier, and they're never used for advertising. [To be confirmed with the final analytics setup before launch.]

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details. Apple tells us only whether your purchase is active.

Permissions

Upbeam asks for alarm and notification permission so alarms can ring. Some optional missions may ask for the camera or motion sensors; that data is processed on your device and never stored or sent anywhere.

Children

Upbeam doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: see the support page.

This draft should be reviewed before launch to match the app's final features and the laws that apply to you.
