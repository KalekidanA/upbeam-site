Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

In plain wordsUpbeam has no account, shows no ads, has no tracking and doesn't collect your data. Your alarms, missions and wake-up history stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your alarms, alarm settings, sounds, mission results, wake-up history and Nightstand settings are stored only on your device. We don't have a server that receives them, so we can't see them. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

The Upbeam app doesn't send usage data, crash reports or identifiers to us or to anyone else. It contains no advertising or tracking code. If that ever changes, we'll update this page first and the change will be anonymous: never your name, email, contacts, location or alarm times.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only checks with Apple whether your purchase is active.

Permissions

  • Alarms. Upbeam asks to schedule alarms so they ring on the Lock Screen, through Silent mode and Focus.
  • Camera (Barcode mission only). The camera is used live to read a barcode. No photos or video are saved; only the barcode number you choose is stored on your iPhone, with that alarm.
  • Motion & Fitness (Steps mission only). Upbeam counts steps while the mission is open. The count isn't stored or sent anywhere.
  • Files and Spotify links (wake-up song). A sound file you pick is copied into Upbeam on your iPhone. A Spotify link is saved on your iPhone and opened in the Spotify app after your alarm; Spotify's own privacy policy applies there.

The waitlist (website)

If you join the Upbeam waitlist on this website, we store your email address, the date you joined and which page you joined from. Nothing else: no cookies, no IP address and no tracking. The list is kept in our own Cloudflare account and isn't sold or shared.

We use it to email you when Upbeam launches, and to send occasional news about Upbeam and other apps we make. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your email at any time at support@getupbeam.com.

Children

Upbeam doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: email support@getupbeam.com or see the support page.
