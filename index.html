Questions

Will Upbeam ring if my iPhone is on Silent?

Yes. Upbeam uses Apple's AlarmKit (iOS 26 and later), which lets alarm apps sound through Silent mode and Focus and appear on the Lock Screen.

Does it work if I close the app?

Yes. Alarms are scheduled with the system, so they ring even if the app is closed or your iPhone has restarted.

Is there a free trial?

No trial is needed: the core alarm clock is free forever. Upgrade only if you want more missions, sounds and history.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 26 or later.

What happens to my data?

It stays on your iPhone. There's no account and no server that stores your alarms or sleep times.
